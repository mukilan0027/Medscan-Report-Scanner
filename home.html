Frequently Asked Questions

Is my medical data safe?

Yes. We process images locally in your browser session before sending them to the secure AI endpoint. We do not store your medical reports on our servers.

Can it read handwritten doctor notes?

Our AI is trained on handwriting recognition (OCR) and can interpret legible doctor's notes, prescriptions, and comments with high accuracy.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. MedScan AI is an informational tool to help you understand your reports. Always consult a certified medical professional for diagnosis and treatment.
